Privacy Policy

Type & Translate - Speed Typing & Live Multilingual Practice
🔒 Client-First Privacy Guarantee — Zero Keystroke Logging

1. Overview

Welcome to Type & Translate (Application Package ID: com.typetranslate.app). We respect your personal privacy and are committed to protecting it through full compliance with this policy.

Type & Translate is designed as a client-first typing practice and multilingual translation web/PWA application. All personal metrics and performance histories remain on your device.

2. Information We Collect

We do not require user account registration, and we do not harvest personal data. The application stores minimal session parameters locally on your browser:

  • Local Performance Telemetry: Words Per Minute (WPM), accuracy percentage, test duration, and completed practice passage counts. Stored strictly in your browser's localStorage.
  • Preferences: User-selected target languages, sound effect toggles, theme settings, and font options.
  • Custom Drills: Any text passages you manually paste or import for practice are saved in local device cache only.

3. No Keystroke Logging Guarantee

We strictly DO NOT log, capture, record, or transmit your raw keystroke sequences onto external marketing servers or databases. All typing speed calculation logic occurs strictly in your device's memory while the test is active.

4. Camera OCR & Microphone Permissions

If you grant camera or microphone access for document scanning or speech practice:

  • Camera Document Scanning: Used exclusively on-demand to perform Optical Character Recognition (OCR) on user-selected book pages or text signs. Images are processed transiently and immediately discarded. No camera footage is saved or stored.
  • Microphone / Speech-to-Text: Used solely to assist pronunciation and speech typing practice. Audio data is evaluated locally in-session.

5. Third-Party Services & Translation API

To provide real-time word translations, the application sends plain-text phrases to secure translation API proxies (such as Google Translate / Gemini API). No Personally Identifiable Information (PII), device identifiers, or user profile information is transmitted with translation requests.

6. Data Control & Deletion

Since all practice logs are stored locally on your device, you hold 100% control over your data. You can erase all stored metrics, test scores, and custom drills at any time by:

  • Clicking "Clear History" inside the app's Scores Log panel.
  • Clearing your web browser cache and site data for this domain.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)

Type & Translate is safe for students of all ages, including children under 13. We do not collect or request any personal identifiers, names, email addresses, or location data from young learners.
